# Safety and human gates

Stop and request an explicit human decision for:

- unknown voltage, current, timing, temperature, mechanical, environmental, or regulatory requirements;
- ambiguous polarity, pin mapping, package, footprint, orientation, or datasheet interpretation;
- a component substitution or supplier-driven design change;
- any waiver or relaxation of an acceptance threshold;
- missing or untrusted models needed for a functional claim;
- unsupported or lossy behavior that can affect intended function;
- mains, high voltage, high power, battery safety, RF, high-speed memory, medical, automotive, aerospace, or regulated designs;
- production publication, purchase, fabrication ordering, or release promotion.

Present the decision as a small packet: the unresolved choice, known alternatives, available evidence, risk, and checks that must be rerun after the decision.

Never hide a missing capability behind prose, loosen a tolerance merely to pass, invent a datasheet value, create a waiver without authorization, or describe pre-compliance evidence as certification.
